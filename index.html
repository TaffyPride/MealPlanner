          Meal Instructions

          Monday: Crispy Air-Fried Chicken Thighs with Steamed Rice & Mixed Veg

          Ingredients:
          1. Prep Chicken: Pat 300g chicken thighs completely dry with kitchen towel. Rub evenly with 1 tbsp cooking oil, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
          2. Air Fry Chicken: Place the chicken thighs in **Zone 1 (Left Drawer)** of your DualZone air fryer. Set to **Air Fry at 200°C for 18–20 minutes**, flipping them halfway through to ensure an extra-crispy skin.
          3. Cook Rice: Rinse 150g white rice under cold water until it runs clear. Combine in a saucepan with 300ml cold water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat, cover tightly with a lid, reduce heat to the lowest setting, and simmer for 10 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave covered to steam for 5 more minutes.
          4. Steam Veg: Steam or boil 150g frozen mixed vegetables for 4–5 minutes until tender. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve alongside the crispy chicken and hot vegetables.

          Tuesday: Broccoli, Bacon & Cheese Pasta Bake

          Ingredients:
          1. Boil Pasta & Broccoli: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add 150g pasta and cook for 5 minutes. Chop 150g broccoli into small florets, add them into the boiling pasta water for the final 3 minutes of cooking, then drain thoroughly.
          2. Fry Bacon: Dice 100g bacon and fry in a pan over medium heat for about 5 minutes until crispy and golden brown. Set aside.
          3. Make Cheese Sauce: In a saucepan, melt 15g butter over medium heat. Stir in 15g plain flour and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly. Gradually whisk in 200ml milk a little at a time to prevent lumps, stirring until smooth and thickened. Turn off the heat and stir in 2 tsp Lea & Perrins and 70g of grated cheddar cheese until fully melted and smooth.
          4. Assemble & Bake: Combine the cooked pasta, broccoli, and crispy bacon with the cheese sauce in an ovenproof or air-fryer safe dish. Scatter the remaining 30g cheddar cheese over the top. Place the dish in **Zone 1** of your air fryer and set to **Air Fry at 180°C for 6–8 minutes** until the cheese is bubbling and golden brown.

          Wednesday: Beef Patties with Garlic Mash, Carrots, Honey Parsnips & Gravy

          Ingredients:
          1. Roast Parsnips: Toss 150g parsnip batons in 1/2 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper. Place them in **Zone 1 (Left Drawer)** and set to **Roast at 190°C for 6 minutes**.
          2. Air Fry Beef Patties & Yorkshires: Shape 250g beef mince into 2 flat patties and season with salt and pepper. Place the beef patties in **Zone 2 (Right Drawer)** and set to **Roast at 190°C for 10–12 minutes** until fully cooked through. Pop 2 frozen Yorkshire puddings into Zone 2 for the final 2 minutes alongside the patties.
          3. Make Garlic Mash: Peel and cube 400g potatoes and boil in salted water for 15 minutes until fork-tender; drain well. Mash with 2 crushed garlic cloves, 20g butter, 30ml milk, salt, and pepper until smooth and creamy.
          4. Boil Veg & Gravy: Boil chopped carrots and 100g broccoli for 6 minutes until tender. Mix 20g beef gravy granules with 250ml boiling water in a jug, stirring until smooth, and serve hot over the meal.

          Thursday: Chicken & Mixed Veg Egg Fried Rice

          Ingredients:
          1. Cook Chicken: Dice 300g chicken thighs into small bite-sized pieces. Place them in **Zone 1** of your air fryer and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 12 minutes** until golden brown and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
          2. Scramble Eggs: In a skillet or pan on the hob, heat a tiny splash of oil, pour in 2 beaten eggs, and scramble quickly until just set. Break them up into small pieces with a spatula.
          3. Stir-Fry Rice & Veg: Add 150g frozen mixed vegetables and 150g cold cooked white rice straight into the pan with the eggs. Increase heat to high and stir-fry vigorously for 3–4 minutes, breaking up any clumps of rice.
          4. Combine & Finish: Toss the cooked chicken back into the skillet along with 15ml soy sauce. Toss everything together over high heat for 2 minutes until piping hot and evenly coated, then serve.

          Friday: Pork Chops, Roast Carrots & Honey Parsnips, Mash & Gravy

          Ingredients:
          1. Roast Veg in Air Fryer: Peel and slice 2 carrots and 150g parsnips into batons. Toss in 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper. Place in **Zone 1 (Left Drawer)** and set to **Roast at 180°C for 6 minutes** to start softening.
          2. Air Fry Pork Chops: Season 2 pork chops generously with salt and pepper. Place the pork chops flat into **Zone 2 (Right Drawer)** and set to **Roast at 190°C for 12–14 minutes**, turning the chops over halfway through to cook evenly. Rest chops for 3 minutes before serving.
          3. Make Mash: Peel and cube 400g potatoes, boil in salted water for 15 minutes until tender, and drain. Mash thoroughly with 20g butter, 30ml milk, salt, and pepper.
          4. Boil Greens & Gravy: Boil 100g broccoli and 100g peas for 4 minutes until vibrant and tender. Whisk 20g beef gravy granules into 250ml boiling water and serve alongside.

          Saturday: Beef, Sweetcorn & Mushroom Cottage Pie

          Ingredients:
          1. Prepare Potato Mash: Peel and cube 400g potatoes and boil in salted water for 15 minutes until tender; drain well. Mash with 15g butter, 30ml milk, salt, and pepper until fluffy. Set aside.
          2. Cook Beef Filling: Heat 1/2 tbsp oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry 200g beef mince and 1 finely diced onion for 5–6 minutes until the meat is browned. Add 2 diced carrots and 100g sliced mushrooms, cooking for another 2–3 minutes until softened.
          3. Thicken Gravy Base: Sprinkle 20g plain flour directly over the beef and vegetable mixture, stirring continuously for 1–2 minutes. Gradually pour in 350ml boiling water whisked with 20g beef gravy granules and 2 tsp Lea & Perrins. Reduce heat and simmer gently for 3–4 minutes until thick and glossy. Stir in 150g sweetcorn and remove from heat.
          4. Assemble & Bake: Transfer the rich beef filling into an air-fryer safe baking dish. Spread the potato mash evenly over the top, roughing up the surface with a fork to create crispy ridges. Place the dish in **Zone 1** of your air fryer and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 10–12 minutes** until the potato peaks are golden brown and crisp.

          Sunday: Air-Fried Bacon, Honey Parsnip & Potato Hash with Fried Eggs

          Ingredients:
          1. Parboil Hash Base: Peel and dice 400g potatoes and 100g parsnips into small, bite-sized cubes. Place in a pan of boiling water for 5 minutes to partially soften, then drain thoroughly on kitchen paper.
          2. Air Fry Hash & Bacon: Place the parboiled potatoes and parsnips in **Zone 1 (Left Drawer)** and the 200g diced bacon in **Zone 2 (Right Drawer)** to keep meat and veg strictly separated. Toss veg with 1/2 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper. Set both zones to **Air Fry at 200°C for 15–18 minutes**, shaking the drawers vigorously every 5 minutes.
          3. Melt Cheese: During the final 2 minutes of cooking, scatter 100g grated cheese evenly across the top of the crispy bacon and potato hash in both drawers, closing the drawers to let it melt into a gooey layer.
          4. Fry Eggs & Serve: While the cheese melts, fry 4 eggs in a skillet on the hob to your liking. Scoop the loaded hash onto plates and top each portion with fried eggs.

          🛒 Week 2 Ingredients List

          Meal Instructions

          Monday: Roast Chicken Thighs with Garlic Mash & Roast Veg

          Ingredients:
          1. Air Fry Chicken & Veg: Place 300g chicken thighs in **Zone 1 (Left Drawer)**, and your sliced carrots and 150g parsnip batons in **Zone 2 (Right Drawer)**. Toss the veg with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper. Set both zones to **Air Fry at 190°C for 20 minutes**, shaking the vegetables halfway through, until the chicken is cooked through and skin is crisp.
          2. Make Garlic Mash & Greens: Boil 400g cubed potatoes for 15 minutes until tender, then drain and mash with 2 cloves crushed garlic, 20g butter, and 30ml milk. Boil 100g broccoli and 100g peas for 4 minutes.
          3. Make Gravy & Serve: Mix 20g chicken gravy granules with 250ml boiling water and serve hot over the roast dinner.

          Tuesday: Cheesy Sausage, Mash & Onion Gravy Bake

          Ingredients:
          1. Cook Sausages & Potatoes: Place 4 pork sausages in **Zone 1** of your air fryer and set to **Air Fry at 180°C for 12–14 minutes** until browned and cooked through, then slice into bite-sized pieces. Meanwhile, boil 400g potatoes for 15 minutes and mash with 20g butter and 30ml milk.
          2. Make Onion Gravy: Thinly slice 2 onions and fry gently in 1 tbsp oil over medium-low heat for 10 minutes until soft and caramelized. Stir in 20g beef gravy granules dissolved in 250ml boiling water and simmer for 2 minutes.
          3. Bake: Layer the sliced sausages and rich onion gravy at the bottom of an air-fryer safe dish. Spread the creamy mash over the top, scatter 80g grated cheese across the surface, place the dish in **Zone 1**, and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 6–8 minutes** until bubbling and golden.

          Wednesday: Honey BBQ Spaghetti Bolognese & Garlic Toast

          Ingredients:
          1. Simmer Bolognese: Brown 250g beef mince and 1 diced onion in a pan for 6 minutes, draining any excess fat. Stir in 1 tin chopped tomatoes, 3 tbsp HP Honey BBQ sauce, 100g frozen peas, and 2 tsp Lea & Perrins. Reduce heat and simmer gently for 15 minutes to thicken.
          2. Cook Spaghetti: Boil 150g spaghetti in salted boiling water for 9–10 minutes until al dente, then drain.
          3. Make Garlic Toast & Serve: Mix 15g softened butter with 1 tsp garlic powder and spread over 2 slices of bread. Place in **Zone 1** of your air fryer and set to **Air Fry at 180°C for 3–4 minutes** until golden and crisp. Serve the honey BBQ bolognese over the spaghetti with the garlic toast on the side.

          Thursday: Cheesy Ham, Egg, Potato & Honey Parsnip Hash

          Ingredients:
          1. Parboil & Air Fry Hash: Dice 400g potatoes and 100g parsnips, parboil in boiling water for 5 minutes, then drain well. Toss with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper. Place in **Zone 1** and set to **Air Fry at 200°C for 12 minutes**, shaking halfway.
          2. Add Ham & Cheese: Dice 150g cooked ham, stir into the hash basket, and cook for 4 more minutes until crispy. Sprinkle 80g grated cheese over the top and let melt for 2 minutes.
          3. Fry Eggs & Serve: Fry 4 eggs in a skillet on the hob and serve them right on top of the loaded hash.

          Friday: Golden Chicken, Egg & Sweetcorn Fried Rice

          Ingredients:
          1. Cook Chicken: Dice 300g chicken thighs or place them in **Zone 1** of your air fryer set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 14 minutes** until golden and cooked through. Remove and chop into small pieces.
          2. Scramble Eggs & Stir-Fry: In a pan on the hob, scramble 2 eggs quickly. Add 150g sweetcorn and 150g cold cooked white rice, turning heat up to high and stir-frying for 3 minutes.
          3. Combine: Mix the cooked chicken and 15ml soy sauce back into the pan, toss well until piping hot, and serve.

          Saturday: Cheesy Beef & Caramelized Onion Rice Skillet

          Ingredients:
          1. Caramelize & Brown: Thinly slice 1 onion and fry in 1 tbsp oil over medium heat for 10 minutes until sweet and caramelized. Add 250g beef mince and cook for 6 minutes until browned; drain any excess fat.
          2. Combine & Melt Cheese: Stir in 150g cold cooked rice and 1 tsp Lea & Perrins, heating through for 3 minutes. Turn off the heat, scatter 80g grated cheese over the top, cover the pan with a lid, and let rest for 2 minutes until fully melted.

          Sunday: Bacon & Mixed Veg Egg Fried Rice

          Ingredients:
          1. Fry Bacon: Dice 100g bacon and fry in a pan over medium heat until crispy; remove and set aside.
          2. Stir-Fry Rice: In the remaining bacon fat and 1 tbsp oil, scramble 2 eggs. Add 150g frozen mixed veg and 150g cold cooked rice, stir-frying on high heat for 4 minutes.
          3. Finish: Toss the crispy bacon back in along with 15ml soy sauce, heat through for 1 minute, and serve.

          Meal Instructions

          Monday: Air-Fried Chicken, Roast Carrots & Parsnips with Cheesy Mash

          Ingredients:
          1. Air Fry Chicken & Root Veg: Place 300g chicken thighs in **Zone 1 (Left Drawer)**, and your sliced carrots and 150g parsnip batons in **Zone 2 (Right Drawer)**. Toss the veg with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper. Set both zones to **Air Fry at 190°C for 20 minutes** until cooked through and tender.
          2. Cheesy Mash & Veg: Boil 400g cubed potatoes for 15 minutes, drain, and mash with 20g butter, 30ml milk, and 60g grated cheddar cheese. Boil 150g mixed veg for 4 minutes and serve together.

          Tuesday: Crispy Pork Chops & Potato Wedges with Gravy

          Ingredients:
          1. Air Fry Wedges & Chops: Cut 400g potatoes into wedges and 150g parsnips into batons, toss with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper, and place in **Zone 1 (Left Drawer)** set to **Air Fry at 200°C for 18–20 minutes**. Add 2 pork chops to **Zone 2 (Right Drawer)** and cook for **12–14 minutes**, turning chops halfway.
          2. Boil Veg & Make Gravy: Boil 150g mixed veg for 4 minutes. Whisk 20g beef gravy granules with 250ml boiling water and serve.

          Wednesday: Ultimate Loaded Cheesy Beef & Potato Hash

          Ingredients:
          1. Parboil & Brown: Parboil 400g diced potatoes for 5 minutes; drain. In a pan, brown 250g beef mince and 1 diced onion for 5 minutes, draining any excess fat.
          2. Air Fry & Melt Cheese: Toss the drained potatoes and cooked beef together with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper. Split evenly across **Zone 1** and **Zone 2** and set to **Air Fry at 200°C for 12–15 minutes** until crispy. Scatter 100g grated cheese over the top and air fry for 2 more minutes to melt.

          Thursday: Cheesy Chicken & Veg Fried Rice

          Ingredients:
          1. Stir-Fry Rice: Dice 300g chicken thighs and fry in 1 tbsp oil until cooked through (8 minutes). Add 150g frozen mixed veg and 150g cold cooked white rice, stir-frying on high heat for 4 minutes with 15ml soy sauce.
          2. Melt Cheese: Turn off the heat, scatter 60g grated cheese over the top of the fried rice, and cover the pan with a lid for 2 minutes to melt.

          Friday: Quick Air-Fryer Macaroni Bacon Bake

          Ingredients:
          1. Boil Pasta & Fry Bacon: Boil 150g macaroni in salted water for 8 minutes; drain. Dice and fry 100g bacon until crispy.
          2. Make Cheese Sauce & Bake: Melt 15g butter, stir in 15g flour, and gradually whisk in 200ml milk until thickened. Stir in 70g cheese until melted. Combine pasta, bacon, and sauce in an air-fryer safe dish, top with the remaining 30g cheese, place in **Zone 1**, and set to **Air Fry at 180°C for 6 minutes**.

          Saturday: Easy Beef Cottage Pie with Potato Topping

          Ingredients:
          1. Make Filling: Fry 200g beef mince and 1 diced onion in 1 tbsp oil for 6 minutes until browned. Stir in 15g gravy granules dissolved in 150ml water and simmer for 3 minutes. Transfer into an air-fryer safe baking dish.
          2. Slice Potatoes & Air Fry: Slice 400g potatoes very thinly, layer them neatly over the beef filling, and brush lightly with oil and salt. Place the dish in **Zone 1** and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 15 minutes** until the potatoes are tender and golden crisp.

          Sunday: Loaded Bacon & Sweetcorn Jacket Potatoes

          Ingredients:
          1. Air Fry Jackets: Prick 2 baking potatoes with a fork, rub skins with oil and salt. Place one potato in **Zone 1** and one in **Zone 2**, and set to **Air Fry at 200°C for 40–45 minutes** until the insides are fluffy and skins are crisp.
          2. Fry Filling & Load: Fry 200g diced bacon until crisp, then stir in 150g sweetcorn for 2 minutes to warm through. Split open the baked potatoes, mash the insides slightly with 15g butter, and load with the bacon, sweetcorn, and 80g cheese.

          Meal Instructions

          Monday: Honey BBQ Chicken Thighs with Rice & Mixed Veg

          Ingredients:
          1. Glaze & Air Fry Chicken: Whisk 30g HP Honey BBQ sauce, 15g honey, 2 minced garlic cloves, 15ml soy sauce, and 1 tbsp oil. Coat 300g chicken thighs thoroughly, place in **Zone 1**, and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 16–18 minutes**, turning halfway.
          2. Cook Rice & Veg: Cook 150g white rice with 300ml water (boil, cover, simmer 10 mins, rest 5 mins). Boil 150g mixed veg for 4 minutes and serve together.

          Tuesday: Pork Chops with Creamy Mash, Roast Veg & Gravy

          Ingredients:
          1. Air Fry Roast Veg & Chops: Toss carrots and 150g parsnip batons in 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, salt, and pepper; place in **Zone 1** and roast at **180°C for 6 minutes**. Add 2 pork chops to **Zone 2** and cook at **190°C for 12–14 minutes**, turning halfway.
          2. Mash & Greens: Boil 400g potatoes for 15 minutes, drain, and mash with 25g butter and 40ml milk. Boil broccoli and peas for 4 minutes, and make gravy.

          Wednesday: Honey BBQ Meatballs Bolognese with Tagliatelle

          Ingredients:
          1. Air Fry Meatballs: Mix 250g beef mince with half of 1 diced onion, salt, and pepper. Shape into small meatballs, place in **Zone 1**, and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 8–10 minutes**.
          2. Sauce & Pasta: Fry remaining onion in 1 tbsp oil for 4 minutes. Add 1 tin chopped tomatoes and 3 tbsp HP Honey BBQ sauce; simmer for 10 minutes, then drop in the cooked meatballs. Boil 150g tagliatelle and mixed veg, then toss together.

          Thursday: Shredded Chicken & Sweetcorn Fried Rice

          Ingredients:
          1. Cook & Shred Chicken: Place 300g chicken thighs in **Zone 1** and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 16 minutes**, then shred using two forks.
          2. Fried Rice: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan, scramble 2 eggs, add 150g sweetcorn and 150g cold cooked white rice, and stir-fry on high heat for 3 minutes. Toss in shredded chicken and 15ml soy sauce to finish.

          Friday: Loaded Cheesy Bacon, Broccoli & Penne Bake

          Ingredients:
          1. Prep Pasta & Bacon: Boil 150g penne and 100g broccoli together in salted water for 8 minutes; drain. Fry 100g bacon until crispy.
          2. Cheese Sauce & Bake: Melt 15g butter, stir in 15g flour, and whisk in 200ml milk until thick. Stir in 70g cheese. Combine pasta, broccoli, bacon, and sauce in an air-fryer safe dish, top with the remaining 30g cheese, place in **Zone 1**, and set to **Air Fry at 180°C for 6 minutes**.

          Saturday: Easy Beef Mince & Cheesy Potato Bake

          Ingredients:
          1. Parboil & Brown: Parboil 400g cubed potatoes for 6 minutes; drain. Fry 200g beef mince and 1 diced onion for 5 minutes, then stir in 15g gravy granules dissolved in 150ml water.
          2. Bake: Layer the mince and potatoes in an air-fryer safe dish, top with 80g cheese, place in **Zone 1**, and set to **Air Fry at 190°C for 10 minutes** until potatoes are soft and cheese is bubbling.

          Sunday: Loaded Bacon & Cheese Fried Rice

          Ingredients:
          1. Fry Bacon & Eggs: Crisp 200g diced bacon in a pan; set aside. In the same pan with 1 tbsp oil, scramble 2 eggs.
          2. Stir-Fry & Melt: Add 150g cold cooked rice and 15ml soy sauce; stir-fry for 3 minutes. Stir the bacon back in. Turn off the heat, scatter 80g cheese over the top, and cover the pan for 2 minutes to let the cheese melt.

          • Chicken Thighs/Breasts: 74°C (75°C target)
          • Beef Mince / Patties: 71°C
          • Pork Chops / Sausages: 63°C – 71°C
          • Beef Steaks (Med): 60°C – 65°C

          Boiling Eggs (Large, Cold) HOB • BOILING WATER

          • Soft / Runny Yolk: 6 minutes
          • Jammy / Medium Yolk: 7–8 minutes
          • Hard Boiled: 10 minutes
          Plunge in cold water immediately after boiling to peel easily.

          Pasta Boiling Times HOB • SALTED WATER

          • Spaghetti / Tagliatelle: 8–10 minutes
          • Penne / Fusilli / Macaroni: 9–11 minutes
          • Pasta Bake Base: Boil 2 mins under package time before baking.

          🍯 Sauces & Glazes Cheat Sheet

          Honey BBQ Glaze & Dip MEAT & RICE

          • Ingredients: 3 tbsp HP Honey BBQ sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp soy sauce, pinch of garlic powder.
          • Method: Whisk together. Brush onto chicken or pork during the last 5 minutes of air frying for a sticky glaze, or use as a dip.

          Umami Pan Gravy ROASTS & PIES

          • Ingredients: 20g beef/chicken gravy granules, 2 tsp Lea & Perrins, 1 tsp soy sauce, 250ml boiling water.
          • Method: Dissolve granules in boiling water, stir in Worcestershire and soy sauce. Simmer in a pan for 2 minutes.

          Honey-Soy Stir-Fry Sauce RICE & STIR-FRY

          • Ingredients: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp garlic powder, 15ml warm water.
          • Method: Mix in a cup. Pour directly into your wok or pan during the final 2 minutes of cooking fried rice.

          Classic Cheese Sauce (Roux) PASTA BAKES

          • Ingredients: 15g butter, 15g plain flour, 200ml milk, 70g grated cheddar, 1 tsp Lea & Perrins.
          • Method: Melt butter, stir in flour (1 min). Gradually whisk in milk until thick. Turn off heat, stir in cheese and Lea & Perrins until melted.

          Garlic Butter Drizzle & Toast SIDES & BREAD

          • Ingredients: 20g softened butter, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt and pepper.
          • Method: Mix together. Spread on bread for quick garlic toast (air fry at 180°C for 3–4 mins), or melt over hot pork chops.
